Uxia operates an Information Security Management System (ISMS) built on ISO/IEC 27001:2022. It covers the information systems that support the design, development, delivery, and maintenance of the Uxia platform, and no part of the platform is excluded from its scope.

## Certifications

| Framework | Status | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Certification in progress | Information security management across the Uxia platform and organization |
| SOC 2 Type II | Certification in progress | Security, availability, and confidentiality of the Uxia service |

Uxia works with an independent firm specializing in data protection and regulatory compliance to implement and maintain the controls both certifications require. A letter confirming our certification status is available on request.

<Note>
  Uxia isn't certified yet under ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2. We'll update this page when certification is achieved.
</Note>

## How the program works

- **Leadership and governance.** Executive leadership is accountable for information security. A Security Committee and a designated Information Security Officer oversee the ISMS.
- **Documented policies.** A complete set of security and privacy policies covers access, cryptography, development, incident response, business continuity, vendors, and people. Policies are reviewed at least once a year.
- **Risk management.** We assess information security risks periodically and track treatment plans for the risks we identify.
- **Internal audits.** An annual internal audit program checks that controls are working as intended. Nonconformities are tracked through to corrective action.
- **Continuous improvement.** Findings from audits, incidents, and reviews feed back into the ISMS.

## Data protection

Uxia processes personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

- When you use Uxia, you're the data controller and Uxia acts as your data processor.
- Every customer can sign our Data Processing Addendum (DPA), which sets out our processing obligations, security measures, subprocessors, and AI training restrictions.
- Transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another recognized transfer mechanism.
- The competent supervisory authority is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

See [Data protection](/trust-center/data-protection) for details.

## Company information

Uxia is operated by **UXIA TESTING SOLUTIONS, S.L.**, a company incorporated in Barcelona, Spain. ENISA (Spain's national innovation agency) certifies Uxia as an innovative startup under Spain's Startup Law (Law 28/2022).
