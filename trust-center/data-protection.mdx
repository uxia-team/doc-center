You own your data. Uxia processes it only to deliver the service, under your instructions and the terms of our Data Processing Addendum (DPA). We never sell, share, or monetize customer data.

## Roles

- **You are the data controller.** You decide what to test and which data enters the platform.
- **Uxia is your data processor.** We process customer data on your documented instructions and for no other purpose.

## Data we process

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account data | Names, email addresses, and company details of your workspace members |
| Test content | Target URLs, prototypes, designs, tasks, and audience criteria |
| Test outputs | Transcripts, screenshots, action logs, insights, and scores |
| Human participant sessions | Screen, microphone, and optional camera recordings from participants you invite or recruit through a [panel partner](/trust-center/human-participants) |
| Billing data | Processed by Stripe. Uxia doesn't store full payment card details. |

## Data residency

Uxia offers two regions: the EU and the US. Your data is stored in the region assigned to your account, and AI inference is available in the same two regions.

- **EU customers** and other non-US customers: infrastructure located in the European Union.
- **US customers**: infrastructure located in the United States.

We don't offer UK-specific hosting or inference.

## Security of your data

- Encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256 or equivalent.
- Isolated per workspace, so other customers can never access it.
- Accessible to Uxia personnel only on a need-to-know basis, under confidentiality obligations.

See [Security controls](/trust-center/controls) for the full list.

## Retention and deletion

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Test content and outputs | For the duration of your agreement. You can delete individual tests at any time. |
| Data after your agreement ends | Deleted or returned within 30 days, at your choice. We confirm deletion in writing on request. |
| Proof-of-concept and pilot content | Deleted within 30 days of the engagement ending, with written confirmation. |
| Backups | Purged within 90 days of deletion from production systems |
| Security and audit logs without customer data | Up to 12 months |

Deletion uses industry-standard secure deletion methods, so deleted data can't be recovered.

## International transfers

Some of our subprocessors are based outside the European Economic Area. Those transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or another recognized transfer mechanism. See [Subprocessors](/trust-center/subprocessors).

## Personal data breaches

If a personal data breach affects your data, we notify you without undue delay and no later than 24 hours after becoming aware of it. The notification describes the nature of the breach, its likely consequences, and the measures we've taken.

## Your rights

We help you respond to data subject requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Data subjects can also contact us directly at [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app), and we forward each request to the relevant customer.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled personal data, you can contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at [aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).

## Minors

Uxia isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly process personal data of minors.
