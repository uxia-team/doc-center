## Incident response

Uxia maintains a documented incident management process that every confirmed incident follows:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect and assess">
    Security events come from automated monitoring and alerting, AI participant transcripts, and reports from staff, customers, and researchers. Each event is analyzed and classified by severity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contain">
    We limit the impact first. For incidents involving AI participants, this means halting affected sessions and revoking session credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eradicate and recover">
    We remove the root cause and restore affected systems to a known-good state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn">
    Every incident gets a post-incident review. Findings lead to corrective actions and, where needed, new controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customer notification

- **Personal data breaches.** We notify affected customers without undue delay and no later than 24 hours after becoming aware of a breach.
- **Service incidents.** Material incidents affecting the service are communicated by email or in-product notice, with updates until they're resolved.
- **Scheduled maintenance.** We give at least 48 hours' notice of planned maintenance expected to cause downtime.

## Business continuity and availability

- **Resilient managed services.** The platform runs on managed cloud services with built-in high availability. The database is deployed as a replica set with automated backups, and file storage is redundant within each region.
- **Backups.** Backups are encrypted and retained on a rolling basis of no more than 90 days.
- **Reversible releases.** Any release can be rolled back to the previous known-good version.
- **Tested plans.** A business continuity plan and disaster recovery runbook are in place and exercised at least once a year.
- **Service levels.** A Service Level Agreement with availability and support commitments is available to contracted customers. See [Security documentation](/trust-center/documents).

## Report a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in Uxia, email [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app) with "Security" in the subject line. Include the steps to reproduce it and any supporting details.

While investigating, please don't access or change other users' data, degrade the service, or share the issue publicly before we've had a chance to fix it.
