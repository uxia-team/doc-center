Uxia uses the subprocessors below to deliver its service. Each one is bound by a data processing agreement with obligations no less protective than those in our Data Processing Addendum, and Uxia remains responsible for their performance.

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Transfer safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** | Cloud infrastructure, compute, and file storage. Data is stored in your account's region (EU or US). | SCCs, DPA |
| **MongoDB Atlas** (MongoDB, Inc.) | Managed database for customer data. Data is stored in your account's region (EU or US). | SCCs, DPA |
| **Google Cloud Platform** | AI model services, including Google Gemini models. Your data isn't used to train Google models. | SCCs, DPA |
| **OpenAI** | AI model services. Your data isn't used to train OpenAI models. | SCCs, DPA |
| **ElevenLabs** | Speech-to-text transcription and AI voice services for human participant sessions. | SCCs, DPA |
| **WorkOS** | Enterprise SSO, SCIM provisioning, and audit logs | SCCs, DPA |
| **Vercel** | Hosting of the web application | SCCs, DPA |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing (PCI DSS compliant) | SCCs, DPA |

## Panel partners

Uxia recruits external participants through Prolific, TestingTime, and Norstat. They aren't subprocessors, because they only connect us with participants and don't process your data. See [External panel participants](/trust-center/human-participants).

## Changes to subprocessors

We give you at least 30 days' written notice before engaging a new subprocessor. You can object on reasonable grounds within that period.

To get the current list or be notified of changes, email [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app).
