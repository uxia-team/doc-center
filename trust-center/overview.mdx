Uxia runs AI-powered usability tests on your websites, prototypes, and designs. To do that, you trust us with product content, test results, and in some cases recordings of human participants. We take that responsibility seriously.

This Trust Center is the central place for information about how Uxia secures your data, which standards we follow, and how we use AI responsibly. It's written for security, privacy, and procurement teams evaluating Uxia.

## Our commitments

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Security first" icon="lock">
    Encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, multi-factor authentication, and continuous monitoring of our cloud environments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance and trust" icon="badge-check">
    An Information Security Management System (ISMS) built on ISO/IEC 27001, with ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 certification in progress.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privacy and transparency" icon="eye">
    GDPR-aligned processing, region-matched data residency in the EU or US, clear retention periods, and a Data Processing Addendum for every customer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Responsible AI" icon="sparkles">
    Your content is never used to train AI models. AI participants are synthetic and only act within the scope you configure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Security at a glance

- **Encryption.** TLS 1.2 or higher in transit and AES-256 at rest.
- **Data residency.** Your data is stored in the region assigned to your account: the EU or the US.
- **No training on your data.** Neither Uxia nor its AI model providers use your content to train or improve AI models.
- **Tenant isolation.** Each workspace is isolated. Your data is never visible to other customers.
- **Breach notification.** We notify affected customers of a personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
- **Enterprise identity.** SSO, SCIM provisioning, and audit logs are available on the Custom plan.
