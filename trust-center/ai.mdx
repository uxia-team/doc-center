Uxia uses large language models to power AI participants and to generate transcripts, insights, and scores. This page explains how we use them responsibly.

## No training on your content

Uxia doesn't use your content to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model. Our Data Processing Addendum also requires that our subprocessors don't:

- Use your data to train, fine-tune, or improve any public, shared, or foundation model.
- Include your data in datasets used for cross-customer learning or benchmarking.
- Use embeddings, metadata, logs, or other data derived from your content to train or evaluate models.

This commitment applies by default. You don't need to opt out, and it survives the end of your agreement.

## AI model providers

Uxia sends test content to AI model providers through their commercial APIs. Our providers are listed on the [Subprocessors](/trust-center/subprocessors) page. Their commercial API terms exclude your data from model training. AI inference is available in the EU or the US. We don't offer UK-specific inference.

- **Limited provider retention.** Providers may keep API data for a short, limited period solely to monitor for abuse. It's then deleted and never used for training.
- **Zero Data Retention.** For enterprise customers on an annual contract, we can enable Zero Data Retention with our AI model providers so that this abuse-monitoring retention doesn't apply. It covers retention by AI model providers. Your tests and results stay in Uxia for the periods in [Data protection](/trust-center/data-protection).
- **Notice of changes.** We give you at least 30 days' notice before engaging a new AI model provider, and you can object on reasonable grounds.

## No cross-customer use

Your content is never exposed to other customers and never influences results generated for them. No learning persists across workspaces.

## AI participants stay in scope

AI participants are synthetic personas, not real people. They operate within the boundaries you set:

- **You control the target.** AI participants act only on the URLs or prototypes you configure and don't browse beyond them.
- **You bound each session.** The task and stop condition you set define how far a session goes, so you can stop before any irreversible action.
- **Inputs are synthetic.** The values AI participants enter are generated, so no real person's data is submitted.
- **Every action is visible.** Each session produces a step-by-step transcript of what the AI participant saw and did.

For practical guidance, see [AI participant restrictions](/account/security/ai-participant-restrictions).

## Recommended setup

- Run tests against staging or UAT environments populated with synthetic data.
- Use dedicated test accounts with only synthetic data for flows that require a login, or an [interactive login session](/trust-center/authenticated-testing#interactive-login-session).
- Upload synthetic documents, not real ones, for upload steps.

## When something goes wrong

We maintain a dedicated incident response playbook for AI participants. If an AI participant behaves outside its configured scope, or real personal data appears in a test output, we stop the affected sessions, contain the issue, delete affected content where needed, and let you know. See [Incident response](/trust-center/incident-response).
