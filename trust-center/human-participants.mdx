When you need human participants and don't have your own, Uxia can recruit them through a partner panel. This page explains what those participants see, the terms they accept, and how their information is handled.

## Our panel partners

Uxia currently recruits external participants through these partners:

- Prolific
- TestingTime
- Norstat

You describe the audience you need, and the panel partner sources participants who match it. See [Human panel](/human-panel) for how to request one.

Panel partners aren't subprocessors. They only connect us with participants and don't process your data. The test links, test content, recordings, and responses are all held by Uxia.

## What participants can see

Recruited participants only see exactly what you set up in your test. That includes:

- The prototypes and URLs you add to the test.
- Any other media you choose to display to them.
- The tasks, instructions, and questions you write.

They can't access your workspace, your other tests, or results from other participants.

Because participants see the material you display, only include content you're comfortable showing to them. For unreleased products, use a staging environment or a prototype with synthetic data.

## Terms participants accept

Participants must agree to terms and conditions before they can take part. You can fully customize these terms for any test, so you can add your own confidentiality or legal wording.

Participants also confirm that they agree to their session being recorded before it starts.

## How participant information is protected

Participant information is always kept private and securely stored:

- **Private to your workspace.** Recordings, transcripts, and responses are accessible only to you and the teammates you invite. They're never visible to other customers.
- **Not shared with panel partners.** Recordings and responses stay in Uxia.
- **Encrypted.** Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256 or equivalent.
- **Stored in your region.** Data is stored in the region assigned to your account: the EU or the US.
- **Never sold or used for training.** Participant data isn't sold, and it isn't used to train AI models.
- **Limited internal access.** Uxia personnel can only access it on a need-to-know basis, under confidentiality obligations.
- **Deleted on a defined schedule.** Participant data follows the retention periods in [Data protection](/trust-center/data-protection), and we help you respond to data subject requests.

Sessions are transcribed by a subprocessor bound by a data processing agreement. See [Subprocessors](/trust-center/subprocessors).
