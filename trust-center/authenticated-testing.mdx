There are two ways to test a product that sits behind a login. You choose one for each test.

## Credentials

You can enter credentials in the purpose-specific **Credentials** section of a Task block.

- Credentials are securely stored and encrypted at rest.
- They're decrypted only in the instances where they're necessary.
- We advise you not to enter sensitive or potentially dangerous credentials, and to rotate them immediately after the test is completed.

See [Tester credentials](/tester-credentials) for setup.

## Interactive login session

You can set up the test state yourself through the Uxia interface, and sign in as you normally would. We capture the logged-in session, so we don't store any credentials.

All AI participants in the test use that logged-in session. It's encrypted while it's stored and deleted automatically one hour after you save it.

See [Interactive login session](/tester-credentials#interactive-login-session) for setup.

## Allowlist and recognize AI participants

We can share our IP addresses with you, so your team can allowlist them and make it easier for AI participants to get past logins and bot protection.

All navigational requests from AI participants carry the same headers, so they can be easily recognized and detected in your logs and security tools.

To get the IP addresses and header details, email [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app).

## Recommended setup

- Test in a staging or UAT environment with synthetic data.
- Use dedicated test accounts with only the permissions the task needs.

For more guidance, see [Responsible AI](/trust-center/ai#recommended-setup).
