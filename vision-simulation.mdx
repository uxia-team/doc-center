---
title: "Vision Simulation"
description: "Compare your frames side by side under color blindness, blurred vision, and field loss to check that your design still works."
---

The **Vision Simulation** tab shows the same frame under two vision conditions, side by side. Use it to check whether your design still communicates when perception changes. It's the fastest way to catch meaning carried by color alone, or contrast that collapses for some of your users.

You find it next to **Results** in the accessibility check block, in the **Analyze** phase.

## Compare two conditions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a frame">
    Click a numbered thumbnail at the top to choose the frame to simulate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a condition for each panel">
    Each panel has its own condition dropdown. The left panel starts on **Normal Vision** and the right panel on **Red-Blind**, so you begin with a baseline next to a simulation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the severity">
    Drag the **Severity** slider next to the dropdown to make the condition milder or stronger, from 1% to 100%. Each panel starts at 50%. The slider is disabled for **Normal Vision**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look closer">
    Click a simulated image to open it at full size.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To compare two conditions directly, set both panels to simulations, for example **Red-Blind** next to **Green-Blind**.

## Conditions

| Group | Condition | What it simulates |
|---|---|---|
| | **Normal Vision** | Standard color perception with full visual clarity |
| **Color Blindness** | **Red-Blind** | Protanopia: difficulty telling red and green apart |
| | **Green-Blind** | Deuteranopia, the most common form of color blindness |
| | **Blue-Blind** | Tritanopia: difficulty with blue and yellow |
| | **Total Color Blindness** | Achromatopsia: seeing only in grayscale |
| **Clarity Issues** | **Cataracts** | A clouded lens: blurred, hazy vision with yellowing |
| | **Astigmatism** | Blurred vision with ghosting |
| | **Presbyopia** | Age-related loss of near focus |
| **Field Loss** | **Glaucoma** | Loss of peripheral vision, like looking through a tunnel |
| | **Macular Degeneration** | Loss of central vision, which affects detail |

## What to look for

- **Color blindness.** Check that status, errors, links, and chart series don't rely on color alone. A red error message should still read as an error in **Total Color Blindness**.
- **Clarity issues.** Check that small text, thin icons, and low-contrast labels stay legible when blurred.
- **Field loss.** Check where key content and actions sit. With **Glaucoma**, content near the edges fades. With **Macular Degeneration**, the center of the screen does.
- **Hierarchy.** Check that the most important element still stands out when color and sharpness change.

When you spot a problem, go back to the **Results** tab and look for the related criterion, such as *Use of Color* (1.4.1) or *Contrast (Minimum)* (1.4.3). See [Criteria evaluation](/criteria-evaluation).

## Good to know

- The simulations are approximations meant to raise awareness. Real experiences of these conditions vary a lot from person to person.
- Simulations run on image frames. Frames Uxia skipped as non-interface content show the same banner as in **Results**.
- The tab needs a browser with WebGL. If WebGL isn't available, the tab shows **WebGL Required**. Try another browser or turn on WebGL in your browser settings.
