Uxia helps teams validate digital experiences faster, without losing the depth of real UX insight.

Instead of spending days or weeks recruiting participants, scheduling sessions, and preparing reports, you can define your target audience, launch a test, and get detailed results in minutes.

Uxia's **AI participants** — synthetic people built from the audience you define — interact with your product, complete tasks, think aloud, answer questions, and surface friction points across the experience. When you need an extra layer of validation, you can run the same test with **human participants** and review both in one place.

You can test live websites, staging builds, coded prototypes, Figma prototypes, and static designs.

## Build tests from blocks

An unmoderated test is made of blocks, and you can combine as many as you need in one test:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Task" icon="mouse-pointer-click" href="/task-block">
    Participants complete a real task on your live website, Figma prototype, or media prototype while thinking aloud.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Survey" icon="clipboard-list" href="/survey-block">
    Ask open-ended, multiple choice, rating, ranking, and budget questions to understand opinions and motivations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Card sorting" icon="layers" href="/card-sorting">
    See how participants group and label your content to shape your navigation and information architecture.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessibility check" icon="person-standing" href="/accessibility-block">
    Audit your screens against WCAG 2.2 AA or AAA — no participants needed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For example, one test can ask participants to complete your checkout, answer a few questions about it, and sort your product categories — while Uxia audits your key screens for accessibility.

## Why teams choose Uxia

### Speed

Traditional user testing can take days or weeks from recruitment to final report. With AI participants, you get results in minutes, so your team can test more often and make decisions faster.

### Cost

Recruiting participants, paying incentives, scheduling sessions, and using multiple research tools quickly becomes expensive. Uxia's credit-based model lets you pay for what you run, and your first test is free.

### Depth

Uxia goes beyond a high-level summary. Each test includes participant profiles, step-by-step think-aloud transcripts, friction points, prioritized insights, and fix suggestions, so you understand both what happened and why.

### Audience control

Define the audience you want to test with, including demographic, psychological, and contextual characteristics, and evaluate your experience from the perspective of the users who matter most.

### AI and human research in one place

Run the same test with AI participants and with real people, and review both in the same results.
