This page covers the mechanics of creating an audience and reviewing its AI participants. For the concepts behind it — the three layers and how AI participants work — see the [Overview](/audiences/overview).

## Where to create an audience

You can create an audience from two places:

- **AI Audience** in the sidebar. Click **Create audience** to build one you can reuse in any test.
- **The Recruit phase of a test.** Open **AI participants** and click **Create audience**. When you finish, the new audience is added to the test.

Each audience is shown as a card with its name, participant count, age range, and gender mix. Its menu lets you **View participants**, **Review filters**, **Rename**, **Clone**, or **Delete**. Cloning copies the criteria and filters but not the generated participants, so you'll generate a fresh set in the copy. It's handy when you want a variation on an existing segment.

## Setting up filters

In the builder, give the audience a name, then switch filters on from the picker on the right; active filters collect on the left. New audiences start with the three core filters already active:

- **Age** — a range anywhere from 18 to 100
- **Gender** — male, female, or both
- **Languages** — defaults to English, with the option to match any or all selected languages

Beyond those, a wider set lets you tighten the segment: country of residence and nationality, tech literacy, education, marital and employment status, industry, role, and household or personal income. There's also a free-text **Custom Details** field for lifestyle, motivations, routines, or other traits. You don't need to fill everything in — anything you leave blank is auto-populated to keep each AI participant coherent.

<Note>
  Advanced filters and Quick Fill are gated by plan. On lower tiers you'll see an **Unlock more audience filters** prompt where they'd otherwise appear.
</Note>

### Quick Fill

If you'd rather not set filters by hand, **Quick Fill** (top right) does it for you in one of two ways:

- **Smart URL** — paste a product or website URL and Uxia scrapes the page, infers who the product is for, and proposes a matching set of filters for you to review and adjust.
- **Manual** — describe your target users in free text (an audience blueprint) and let Uxia translate that into filters.

If you continue with no filters set, Uxia warns that it will generate random participants.

## Generating AI participants

When your filters are ready, click **Populate audience** and confirm. Uxia saves the audience and starts generating AI participants that match your criteria in the background.

<Warning>
  Once participants are generated, the audience's filters and details are **locked**. To change them, create a new audience — cloning an existing one is the quickest way to reuse the same criteria.
</Warning>

To review the result, choose **View participants** on the audience card. Each AI participant appears as a card with two views:

- a **demographic / lifestyle** view — nationality, country, languages, tech literacy, education, industry, role, employment, income, and a short description. Fields you set are styled differently from the ones Uxia filled in.
- a **psychological profile** view — the OCEAN radar chart plus a narrative of the participant's motivations and decision-making style

AI participants built with enrichment carry an **Enriched** badge. If one isn't quite right, **Replace tester** swaps it for a fresh profile that still matches all your criteria. **Review Audience Filters** opens a side panel with the filters the audience was built from.

## Choosing participants for a test

You pick who runs a test in the Recruit phase, not in the audience itself. Include up to three audiences, then either:

- use **Distribute evenly** to split the participant count equally across audiences, or
- use **Handpick participants** to choose specific AI participants from each audience.

The number of AI participants you can run depends on your plan. See [AI participants](/ai-participants) for the full flow.
