Audience Enrichment is a custom service that builds AI participants directly from your own data. Instead of relying on a representative default panel, you feed Uxia the audience knowledge you already have — personas, ICP definitions, interviews, research — and it generates AI participants that reflect _your_ users, so the feedback maps directly onto your product and business.

## When to use it

Enrichment pays off most when your user persona or ICP is already well mapped — when you understand your users' pains, needs, goals, and end-to-end experience in detail. A B2B tool for compliance officers at mid-market financial firms has a very different user than a consumer fitness app; when your audience has distinct workflows, vocabulary, risk tolerances, or trust patterns, a generic panel can miss the nuances that matter most. Enrichment closes that gap and makes the resulting feedback far more relevant.

## What you can provide

Uxia accepts a range of inputs, and the more specific and detailed they are, the more precisely it can configure your AI participants:

- **Long-form interviews** — user interview recordings or transcripts
- **Persona definitions** — narrative or structured personas, including through one of our approved templates
- **ICP definitions** — firmographic and psychographic descriptions of your ideal customer
- **Audience segments** — breakdowns by behavior, lifecycle stage, or use case
- **Behavioral and research data** — anonymized usage patterns, onboarding analytics, support-ticket themes, or past survey and usability findings

Enrichment can also layer in extra context so AI participants reason more like your real users — for example, **knowledge of your competitors** or **previous versions of your product**, so feedback accounts for what your audience is already familiar with.

## How Uxia uses your data

Uxia doesn't simply quote your documents back as participant behavior. Your inputs are combined with Uxia's behavioral frameworks and public demographic data to produce profiles that are both grounded in your audience reality and behaviorally coherent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your audience data">
    Share your documents with the Uxia team — interviews, personas, ICP definitions, segment data, or research outputs. Uxia accepts common document formats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uxia generates candidate profiles">
    The enrichment engine analyzes your inputs and produces AI participant profiles tailored to the audience you've described — each with demographics, behavioral patterns, and psychographic traits derived from your data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and choose your AI participants">
    You see the generated profiles before anything runs. Enriched profiles carry an **Enriched** badge. Inspect each one, confirm it fits your intent, and use **Handpick participants** in the Recruit phase to choose the lineup for your test. You're never locked into an auto-assigned panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your test">
    Your enriched AI participants run the test, and the resulting transcripts and insights reflect how your actual target audience would experience your product.
  </Step>
</Steps>
