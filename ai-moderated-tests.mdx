An **AI-moderated test** is a live interview with a real participant, run by an AI moderator instead of a researcher. Where an [unmoderated test](/unmoderated-tests) lets participants work through your blocks on their own, an AI-moderated test is a conversation: the moderator follows your research guide, asks follow-up questions, and digs deeper when an answer is interesting or unclear.

## How it will work

1. **Describe what you want to learn.** Tell Uxia what you want to research, and let it generate a study for you — or build the research guide yourself.
2. **Shape the research guide.** Set your research goals, the questions and topics to cover, and how the moderator should follow up.
3. **Interview real participants.** The AI moderator runs each session live, by voice, adapting to what each participant says.

## When to use it

- You need the depth of a one-on-one interview, at the scale and speed of an unmoderated study.
- You want to explore motivations, expectations, and decisions — not only observe behavior.
- You don't have the time or researchers to moderate every session yourself.
