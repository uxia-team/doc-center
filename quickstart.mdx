This walkthrough takes you from sign-up to your first results with an unmoderated test and AI participants.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [platform.uxia.app](https://platform.uxia.app/) and sign up with your email, Google, or Figma account. You'll land on **Home**, where your projects, tests, and audiences live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a test">
    Click **Create test**, choose **Unmoderated test**, give it a title, and optionally add it to a project. Click **Create test** to open the builder. See [Build your test](/build-your-test).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Task block">
    In the **Add a block** picker, choose the task type that matches what you want to test:

    - **Live website task** — a live site, staging build, or coded prototype, by URL.
    - **Figma prototype task** — a Figma prototype share link.
    - **Media prototype task** — a clickable prototype you build from your own images.

    Then fill in the essentials:

    - **Task** — the one concrete thing participants should do, such as *"Buy a monthly metro pass using a credit card."* See [Task](/mission).
    - **Scenario** — who the participant is and why they're here. See [Scenario](/test-scenario).
    - **Stop condition** — what's on screen when the task is done (live website and Figma tasks). See [Stop condition](/stop-condition).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more blocks (optional)">
    Add a **Survey** after the task to ask follow-up questions, a **Card sorting** block to test your navigation labels, or an **Accessibility check** of your key screens. See [Survey block](/survey-block), [Card sorting block](/card-sorting), and [Accessibility check block](/accessibility-block).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recruiting">
    Click **Start recruiting**. In **AI participants**, select an audience — or click **Create audience** to build one — and choose how many participants to include. See [AI participants](/ai-participants) and [Building an audience](/audiences/building-an-audience).

    <Tip>
      Paste your product URL into the audience builder's Quick Fill and Uxia suggests an audience profile for your product and industry.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the test">
    Check the credit estimate and click **Launch test**. Uxia reviews your setup in a few seconds and flags anything unclear before participants start. See [Recruit participants](/recruit-participants).

    There's no recruitment delay, no no-shows, and no scheduling. You can leave the page — we'll email you when your results are ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze your results">
    Results appear in the **Analyze** phase as participants finish. For each task, you get:

    - **Insights** — ranked usability issues with evidence and fix suggestions, plus the Usability Optimization Score (UOS). See [Insights and UOS](/results).
    - **Transcripts** — each participant's step-by-step think-aloud journey. See [Transcripts](/test-transcripts).
    - **Analytics and journeys** — success rate, drop-off, misclicks, heatmaps, and paths. See [Analytics and journeys](/analytics).

    Use [Ask AI](/ask-ai) to question your results, and [share or export](/share-and-export) them with your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
