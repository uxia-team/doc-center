title: "UXIA-Q Score"
---

The **UXIA-Q Score** is a standardized metric used to measure the overall quality of a website or digital product's user experience.

Each tester evaluates the experience through **8 questions across 4 key pillars: Usability, Trust, Appearance, and Loyalty**. The responses are combined into a single score, giving teams a consistent way to evaluate and compare digital experiences.

## What does the UXIA-Q Score measure?

The UXIA-Q framework evaluates four key aspects of the experience:

- **Usability:** How easy the product is to use and navigate.
- **Trust:** How credible the information feels and how confident users would feel conducting business through the product.
- **Appearance:** How attractive the experience is and how clean the overall presentation feels.
- **Loyalty:** How likely users would be to return to the product or recommend it to others.

These pillars are measured through 8 standardized questions:

**Usability**

- Easy to use
- Easy to navigate

**Trust**

- Credible information
- Confident conducting business

**Appearance**

- Attractive
- Clean presentation

**Loyalty**

- Likely to return
- Likely to recommend

## How Uxia applies the UXIA-Q framework

For synthetic testing, Uxia adapts these questions so testers evaluate them based on the persona they represent, the task they performed, and what they actually experienced while navigating the product.

Uxia also applies additional techniques to reduce common LLM response biases and improve consistency across testers. Responses are generated in the context of the full testing session rather than evaluating each question in isolation.

This provides a standardized quantitative measure of the experience that complements the behavioral observations, issues, and qualitative feedback identified during the test.
