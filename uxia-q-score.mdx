The **UXIA-Q Score** is a standardized metric that measures the overall quality of a website or digital product's user experience.

Participants evaluate the experience through **8 questions across 4 pillars: Usability, Trust, Appearance, and Loyalty**. The answers are combined into a single score, giving teams a consistent way to evaluate and compare digital experiences.

## Turn on UXIA-Q

On a [Task block](/task-block), switch on **Measure user experience with UXIA-Q**. Each Task block has its own toggle, so you can measure some flows and not others.

- **Cost:** +1 credit per AI participant, on top of the Task block's 20 credits.
- **Plans:** paid plans only. On the Free plan, the toggle shows an **Upgrade** badge.
- **Results:** the **Benchmarks** tab of the Task block, in the Analyze phase. See [Benchmark results](/benchmark-results).

You can switch on [SUS](/sus) in the same block for another +1 credit per AI participant.

## What does the UXIA-Q Score measure?

The UXIA-Q framework evaluates four aspects of the experience:

- **Usability:** how easy the product is to use and navigate.
- **Trust:** how credible the information feels and how confident users would feel doing business through the product.
- **Appearance:** how attractive the experience is and how clean the overall presentation feels.
- **Loyalty:** how likely users would be to return to the product or recommend it to others.

These pillars are measured through 8 standardized questions, each rated on a 5-point scale from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree:

**Usability**

- Easy to use: The product was easy to use
- Easy to navigate: It was easy to navigate within the product

**Trust**

- Credible information: The information on the product is credible
- Confident conducting business: I feel confident conducting business with this product

**Appearance**

- Attractive: The product is attractive
- Clean presentation: The product has a clean and simple presentation

**Loyalty**

- Likely to return: I would likely return to this product
- Likely to recommend: I would recommend this product to a friend or colleague

The overall UXIA-Q Score is the average of the four pillar scores.

## How Uxia applies the UXIA-Q framework

Uxia adapts these questions so AI participants answer them based on the persona they represent, the task they performed, and what they actually experienced while navigating the product.

Uxia also applies additional techniques to reduce common LLM response biases and improve consistency across participants. Answers are generated in the context of the full session rather than evaluating each question in isolation.

This provides a standardized, quantitative measure of the experience that complements the behavioral observations, issues, and qualitative feedback from the test.
