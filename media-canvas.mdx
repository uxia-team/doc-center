A **Media prototype task** lets you test designs that aren't built yet. You upload your screens as images, connect them into a flow on the canvas, and participants navigate it like a real interface.

## When to use it

Reach for a media prototype when you're testing:

- Static screens exported from Figma, Sketch, or any design tool.
- A set of images representing a flow that isn't built yet.
- Early design iterations, before you have a working prototype.

A media prototype accepts images only. To test a Figma prototype by its link, use a [Figma prototype task](/source) instead.

## Build the prototype

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the canvas">
    On the block, click **Add prototype**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a mode">
    In **Select mode**, choose **Interactive** or **Sequential**, then click **Continue**. See [Modes](#modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your designs">
    Click **Import designs** and choose your images. Add more later with **Import Files** in the **Frames** sidebar. A prototype holds up to 35 frames.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the flow">
    In an interactive flow, choose the starting frame, then the final frame, and connect the frames with interaction areas. In a sequential flow, drag the frames in the **Frames** sidebar into the order participants see them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Open the **Preview** tab to click through an interactive flow as a participant would. Then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The run ends when participants reach the final frame, so a media prototype task has no stop condition. You still write a [Task](/mission) and a [Scenario](/test-scenario), as in any Task block.

## Modes

| Mode | How it works | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Interactive** (Recommended) | An interactive prototype with clickable areas and different paths. | You want a realistic flow with branches. |
| **Sequential** | A linear sequence of frames that participants view in order. | You want a simple, quick walkthrough. |

## Interactive flows

An interactive flow needs at least 2 frames. After you import them, Uxia asks you to pick the starting frame and then the final frame, which are marked **Start** and **End**.

To connect frames, click the target icon in the toolbar (**Create interaction areas**), draw an area on a frame, and connect it to the frame it leads to. Then choose the action that triggers it.

A to-do list on the canvas tracks what's left: a start and an end, at least one outgoing connection on every frame, and a path from every frame to the end. The **Preview** tab is available once a start frame is set.

## Actions

| Action | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Click** | Buttons, links, or any element that moves to another frame when clicked. |
| **Input** | Text fields or search bars. Simulates typing before moving to the next frame. |
| **Scroll / Swipe** | Long pages, carousels, or swipeable content. Set the direction: up, down, left, right, or any. |
| **Wait** | Frames that change automatically after a delay. Set the wait time in milliseconds. |
| **Drag** | Sliders or objects the user must drag from one point to another. |
| **Hover** | Elements that reveal extra information or change when the cursor moves over them. |

## Canvas controls

- **Click mode.** When enabled, drag frames to reposition them. When disabled, drag to move around the canvas.
- **Create interaction areas.** Draw interactive regions on a frame and connect them to the next step. Interactive mode only.
- **Zoom.** Use the zoom menu in the toolbar (zoom in, zoom out, 50%, 100%, 200%), pinch on a trackpad, or scroll while holding Ctrl or Cmd.
- **Fit all images in view.** Adjust the view to show every frame on the canvas.
- **Pan.** Scroll with your trackpad or mouse wheel, drag the canvas when click mode is off, or hold the **Spacebar** and drag.

In the **Frames** sidebar, you can also rename, remove, and jump to frames.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a full-screen scroll?">
    Create an interaction area that covers the entire frame, give it the **Scroll / Swipe** action, and connect it to the next frame showing the scrolled view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I simulate text input?">
    Create an **Input** interaction over the empty field and connect it to a frame where the field appears filled in. For forms with several fields, you can fill them all in one step, or create separate frames for a more realistic experience.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the order of frames matter?">
    In a sequential flow, yes: participants see the frames in the order of the **Frames** sidebar. In an interactive flow, no: the path is defined by the interaction areas and their connections, not by frame numbers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do participants see transition effects?">
    No. The transition animations in **Preview** are only for building and reviewing the prototype. Participants experience only the interactions and the resulting frames.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
