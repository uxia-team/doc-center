## Two test types

- **Unmoderated tests** — participants complete the test on their own. You combine blocks into a single test and run it with AI participants, human participants, or both. See [Unmoderated tests](/unmoderated-tests).
- **AI-moderated tests** — an AI moderator interviews real participants live, following your research guide and asking follow-up questions. See [AI-moderated tests](/ai-moderated-tests).

## Blocks

| Block | What participants do | What you learn |
|---|---|---|
| [Task](/task-block) | Complete a task on your live website, Figma prototype, or media prototype while thinking aloud | Where people succeed, struggle, or drop off — and why |
| [Survey](/survey-block) | Answer open-ended, multiple choice, rating, ranking, and budget questions | Opinions, preferences, and motivations |
| [Card sorting](/card-sorting) | Group cards into categories | How people expect your content to be organized |
| [Accessibility check](/accessibility-block) | No participants — Uxia audits your screens | Which WCAG 2.2 criteria pass, fail, or need review |

## Participants

- **AI participants** are AI-generated people built from an [audience](/audiences/overview): demographics, a psychological profile, and optionally your own research data. They're available on demand, and a test with AI participants usually finishes within minutes.
- **Human participants** are real people. Invite your own users with [test links](/test-links), or ask our team to recruit a [human panel](/human-panel). Their sessions are recorded so you can watch them back.

Both take exactly the same test, so you can combine them in one study and review their results side by side.

## How AI participants experience your product

Each AI participant works through your test independently, the way a person with their profile would:

1. It reads the [scenario](/test-scenario) — the situation it's in and why it's there.
2. It looks at each screen, decides what to do next, and acts: clicking, typing, scrolling, and navigating toward the [task](/mission).
3. At every step, it records what it notices, what it expects to happen, and why it acted — the think-aloud [transcript](/test-transcripts).
4. It stops when it reaches the [stop condition](/stop-condition) or the final frame of your prototype, or when it gives up.
5. It answers any survey questions and card sorts that follow.

Uxia then analyzes all sessions together to produce insights, metrics, and fix suggestions.

<Note>
  AI participants can't complete some flows — for example, social logins, verification codes, or two-factor authentication. See [Flows AI participants can't complete](/unsupported-flows).
</Note>

## The test lifecycle

Every unmoderated test moves through **Build › Recruit › Analyze**:

1. **[Build](/build-your-test)** — add and configure blocks.
2. **[Recruit](/recruit-participants)** — choose participants and launch.
3. **[Analyze](/analyze-results)** — review results block by block, chat with your results, and share them.

## Credits

AI participants and accessibility checks use credits. The cost depends on the blocks in your test and the number of AI participants — for example, each task costs 20 credits per AI participant. Human participants you invite with test links don't use credits. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits).
