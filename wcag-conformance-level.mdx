title: "WCAG Conformance Level"
description: "Choose whether the accessibility check audits your screens against WCAG 2.2 Level AA or Level AAA."
---

The conformance level sets which WCAG 2.2 success criteria Uxia checks your screens against. You choose it in the **WCAG Conformance Level** field at the top of the [Accessibility check block](/accessibility-block).

## The two options

| Option | Level | In short | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recommended** (default) | AA | Addresses the most common barriers | Industry and legal baseline. Required by most accessibility regulations (EU, US, UK, etc). |
| **Advanced** | AAA | Highest level of accessibility coverage | Best for inclusive experiences. Rarely required by law. |

Click an option to select it. The line below the options explains the level you picked. **Learn more** opens the W3C's overview of WCAG in a new tab.

## Levels are cumulative

WCAG defines three levels: A, AA, and AAA. Each criterion belongs to exactly one level, and each level includes the ones below it:

- An **AA** audit checks all Level A and Level AA criteria.
- An **AAA** audit checks all Level A, AA, and AAA criteria.

The results show what was checked in the summary card, for example **Conformance Level Tested: A & AA**. You can also filter the results by **Level** to focus on one level at a time. See [Criteria evaluation](/criteria-evaluation).

## Which level to choose

**Choose AA for most products.** It's the level that most accessibility laws and policies point to, and the one most teams work toward. Leave **Recommended** selected unless your team explicitly needs AAA.

**Choose AAA when you aim higher.** Pick **Advanced** when you're building for the most inclusive experience, or when you work to a stricter internal standard. Expect more **Failed** and **Review** items: some AAA criteria are hard to meet for every kind of content, which is why laws rarely require AAA.

<Tip>
  WCAG 2.2 builds on WCAG 2.1. Screens that meet WCAG 2.2 at a given level also meet WCAG 2.1 at that level, so a 2.2 audit still works when a regulation refers to 2.1.
</Tip>

## Good to know

- The level doesn't change the cost. The block costs 10 credits per frame at either level.
- You can switch levels at any time while you build. The level is fixed once you launch.
- The check doesn't tell you which law applies to your product. Confirm your legal requirements with your legal or compliance team.
