---
title: "Criteria Evaluation"
description: "Read accessibility check results frame by frame, filter the WCAG criteria, and work through fixes and manual verification."
---

When the audit finishes, open the **Analyze** phase and select the accessibility check block in the sidebar. The block has two tabs: **Results**, covered here, and [**Vision Simulation**](/vision-simulation).

## Pick a frame

Thumbnails of your frames run along the top. Click one to select it; the summary and the table then show results for that frame only. Click the selected thumbnail again to open a larger preview.

If a frame isn't a digital interface, Uxia skips it and shows a banner instead of results: **Photograph Detected**, **Artwork Detected**, **Document Detected**, or **Non-UI Content Detected**.

## The summary card

The summary card in the block header shows **Conformance Level Tested**, for example *A & AA*, followed by three counts:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Passed** | The criterion is met, or doesn't apply to this frame |
| **Failed** | The criterion is not met |
| **Review** | A person needs to check the criterion to confirm conformance |

Click a count to filter the table to that status. Click it again to clear the filter. The info icon next to the counts shows how many Failed and Review items fall under each **Review Scope**. When every criterion in the frame passes, the card shows **Fully Compliant** instead.

## Filter the criteria

Above the table:

- **Search success criteria...** finds criteria by code, name, level, or category.
- **Status** filters by Passed, Failed, or Review.
- **Review Scope** filters by **Design** or **Code**.
- **Level** filters by A, AA, or AAA.

The counter on the right shows **Showing X of Y criteria**. When filters are active, the summary card counts only the matching criteria.

## Read the table

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **WCAG Code** | The criterion number, for example 1.4.3 |
| **Success Criterion** | Its name, for example Contrast (Minimum) |
| **WCAG Level** | A, AA, or AAA |
| **Category** | The WCAG principle: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, or Robust |
| **Status** | Passed, Failed, or Review |
| **Review Scope** | Where the issue or fix lives, for Failed and Review items |

**Review Scope** tells you who should act. **Design** means the issue or fix is in the design layer: layout, contrast, copy, sizing. **Code** means it's in the implementation: semantics, labels, keyboard behavior. Some criteria need review in both.

## Open a criterion

Click a row, or **See More**, to open the **Success criterion details** drawer. It shows:

- A plain-language description of the criterion, with its status, level, scope, and category.
- The frame. If Uxia located the elements involved, switch on **Show Affected Elements** to outline them. Click the frame to enlarge it.
- **How To Fix** for a Failed criterion, **How To Verify** for one in Review, or **Guidance** for one that passed. When available, Uxia's **Findings:** for this frame come first.
- A **Checklist** of the steps to fix or verify the criterion.
- A **Contrast Checker** for the contrast criteria (1.4.3, 1.4.6, and 1.4.11). Pick the text or element color and the background color from the frame, or type their hex codes. The checker shows the contrast ratio, **Pass** or **Fail**, and the required ratio.
- **WCAG Criteria**, which opens the W3C's explanation of the criterion.

<Tip>
  The Contrast Checker applies the requirement for normal-size text. Large text only needs 3:1 at Level AA and 4.5:1 at Level AAA.
</Tip>

## Mark criteria as passed

Once you've checked a Failed or Review item yourself, mark it as passed in either of these ways:

- Click **Mark as Passed** in the table row or in the drawer.
- Tick every item in the drawer's **Checklist**. Uxia confirms with *"All verification items completed. Success criteria passed!"*

The criterion then shows as **Manually Verified** in the drawer. To undo it, click **Reset Status**. Manual changes apply to the selected frame only, and they're saved with the results.

<Tip>
  Work through the results as a checklist. Clear the **Failed** items first, then split the **Review** items between designers and engineers using the scope labels.
</Tip>

## Share and discuss results

- People who open a [shared results link](/share-and-export) see the same results, read-only. They can't mark criteria or tick the checklist.
- The PDF report (Pro and Custom plans) can include the audit. By default it lists the Failed and Review items.
- [Ask AI](/ask-ai) can answer questions about the audit and link to specific criteria. Click a criterion card to open its details.

<Note>
  **Fully Compliant** means every criterion Uxia evaluated passed for that frame. It's not a legal audit or a certification.
</Note>
