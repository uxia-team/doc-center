There are two ways to use Figma with Uxia. You can sign in to Uxia with your Figma account, and you can test a Figma prototype by pasting its link into a **Figma prototype task**. Testing a prototype doesn't require a Figma login or any Figma connection.

## Sign in to Uxia with Figma

Figma is available as a social login option. When you create an account or sign in to Uxia, click **Continue with Figma** to use your Figma login instead of creating a separate Uxia password.

This is separate from testing a prototype. You can use Figma as your login provider without testing anything from Figma, and you can test Figma prototypes without signing in with Figma.

## Test a Figma prototype

A Figma prototype task lets participants complete a task in your prototype while thinking aloud. Uxia opens the prototype from its link, so it must be publicly accessible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the prototype publicly">
    In Figma, open the prototype and set its sharing so **anyone with the link** can view it. Copy the prototype link. It starts with `https://www.figma.com/proto/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Figma prototype task">
    In the Build phase of your test, add a **Figma prototype task** block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prototype link">
    Paste the link, choose a viewport, and let Uxia verify it. If the prototype isn't publicly accessible, Uxia tells you so. Update the sharing settings in Figma and verify again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the block and recruit">
    Write the [Task](/mission), [Scenario](/test-scenario) and [Stop condition](/stop-condition), then move on to the Recruit phase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Because the prototype is public, both AI participants and human participants can open and use it. See [Live website and Figma links](/source) for the details of link setup, viewports and verification.

## Test static Figma screens

If your design isn't a clickable Figma prototype yet, export the frames from Figma as images and upload them to a **Media prototype task**. In the prototype canvas, you link the screens together to build a clickable flow. See [Media prototype](/media-canvas).

## Which option should you use?

- **Continue with Figma** when you want to create or access your Uxia account with your Figma credentials.
- **Figma prototype task** when you already have a working Figma prototype and want participants to test it as it is.
- **Media prototype task** when you have static screens and want to build the flow in Uxia.

## Tips and best practices

- Before you start recruiting, check that the prototype opens for someone who isn't signed in to Figma.
- Point the link at the starting frame of the flow you want to test, so participants begin in the right place.
- Keep the prototype focused on the flow in your Task. Extra pages and flows make it easier for participants to wander.
