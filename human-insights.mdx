When people take your test through a [test link](/test-links) or a [human panel](/human-panel), their sessions appear in the [Analyze](/analyze-results) phase. Each session includes a screen recording, the participant's voice, an automatic transcript, and their answers, so you can see real behavior next to what your AI participants did.

## Switch to human results

At the top of Analyze, open the participant type menu and choose **Human participants**. Choose **AI participants** to switch back. The two views are separate: there's no merged view, so switch between them to compare.

In human mode, the filter next to the menu lists your test links with the number of participants from each. Select the links you want and click **Filter**. The selection applies to every block.

If you haven't created a link yet, a block shows **No links created yet** with a **Create link** button.

Accessibility check blocks have no human results, because participants don't take them.

## Task blocks

The **Sessions** tab opens with a row of metrics:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Testers** | Participants included in the metrics. Failed recordings are excluded. |
| **Success Rate** | Share of sessions that completed the flow. |
| **Drop-off** | Share of sessions that abandoned the flow. |
| **Misclick Rate** | Share of clicks outside valid interactive areas. Not shown for live websites. |
| **Avg. Duration** | Average time of successful sessions. |

Below, a table lists each session with **Clips**, **Participant**, **Status**, **Task duration**, **Responded At**, and **Actions**.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | The participant finished the flow and answered every question. |
| **Abandoned** | The participant left the flow before finishing it. |
| **Missing questions** | The participant finished, but left some questions unanswered. |
| **Processing** | The recording or transcript is still being prepared. |
| **Recording failed** | The session couldn't be captured. |
| **Transcript failed** | The recording exists, but the transcript couldn't be generated. |
| **In progress** | The participant is still taking the test. |

Under **Actions**, **Download clip** saves the **Screen view** or **Camera view** recording, and **Delete recording** permanently removes the session and its data.

Click a session to open the player. The **Transcripts** tab follows the video; click a line to jump to that moment. Select text to highlight it and click **Add note** to save it. Your notes appear in the **Notes** tab.

For media prototype tasks, a **Frames** tab shows each screen with participants' clicks as a heatmap, plus the misclick rate and average time on that screen.

## Survey blocks

Survey results have two tabs, as they do for AI participants. **Responses** groups answers by question, and **Participants** shows each person's answers. Next to each answer, **Play recording** opens the session at the moment the participant answered. See [Survey results](/survey-results).

## Card sorting blocks

Human card sorts use the same results views as AI participants. See [Card sorting results](/card-sorting-results).

## Plan limits

| Plan | Human sessions in results |
|---|---|
| Free | Not available |

On Pro, sessions after the first 10 are blurred with **Upgrade to unlock**.

## Why combine AI and human participants

<Tip>
  AI participants give you results in minutes and let you run as many rounds as you need. Human participants add emotional depth, lived context, and the unpredictable behavior only real people bring. Use AI participants to iterate fast and narrow down issues. Then use human sessions to confirm findings, surface edge cases, and build stakeholder confidence.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to run AI participants first?">
    No. You can recruit human participants on any test from the [Recruit](/recruit-participants) phase, with or without AI participants.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can participants use any device?">
    Participants need a desktop browser that supports screen recording. Screen and microphone capture happen in the browser, with no downloads. The face camera is only required when the test link asks for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How are transcripts generated?">
    Automatically, from the audio of each session. A session shows **Processing** until its recording and transcript are ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many human participants should I include?">
    It depends on your goals. For usability testing, five to eight participants usually surface the most actionable issues. To compare segments, recruit more, and use one test link per segment so you can filter by it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
