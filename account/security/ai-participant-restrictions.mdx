AI participants are the synthetic participants Uxia runs against your product. They only ever act on the targets you give them and within the boundaries of the test you configure — you decide what they can reach, which environment they touch, and how far a session is allowed to go. This page covers the controls you have for keeping AI participants tightly scoped.

## Participants only act on the target you configure

Each Task block runs against a target that you provide, and participants stay within it.

- **One target per Task block.** Each Task block runs against its own URL, Figma prototype, or media prototype. Participants navigate that target only.
- **No wandering off.** Participants do not crawl beyond your targets, follow links out to unrelated domains, or fetch external URLs you haven't configured.
- **Limited blast radius.** Because a session is confined to the product you intend to test, there's no exposure to systems or sites outside that scope.

## Test in staging or UAT

You choose the environment participants run against, so you can keep every session away from production.

- **You pick the endpoint.** Point participants at a staging, UAT, or pre-production URL and that is the only place they operate.
- **Production stays untouched.** Since participants act only on the environment you provide, testing a non-production build keeps sessions away from live systems and real user data.
- **Seeded data.** We recommend staging or UAT with seeded, non-production data for any flow that reads or writes sensitive information.

## Bound what a session can do

The [Task](/mission) and [stop condition](/stop-condition) you set in each Task block define how far a participant goes.

- **Scope the Task** to the specific action you want observed. Participants act to complete that task and nothing more.
- **Set a stop condition** that ends the session at a point you choose. If you don't want participants completing an action that writes data — submitting a form, placing an order, changing a setting — stop the session before that step, for example at the review or confirmation screen.
- **Interaction stays on the target.** Participants only fill in and submit forms on the product you're testing, and only when the Task calls for it. They never submit data to external sites.

## Keep a test observational

When you want participants to look but not commit changes:

- Design the Task around navigation and evaluation rather than completing a transactional step, and use a stop condition that ends before any submit or confirm action.
- When a flow does need to write data — carts, bookings, sign-ups, settings — give each participant its own dedicated account so sessions stay isolated and never collide. See [Tester Credentials](/tester-credentials).

## Scope credentials and files

- **Credentials** for gated flows are scoped to the test they're added to and encrypted at rest. The AI participant receives them only to type them into the matching fields. Use dedicated test accounts, and rotate or remove them after a run. See [Tester Credentials](/tester-credentials).
- **Files** you provide for upload steps are scoped to the test. The AI participant sees only each file's name and type; the browser attaches the file itself. Use synthetic documents rather than real ones. See [Tester Files](/tester-files).

## Full visibility into every action

Every AI participant produces a step-by-step transcript of what it saw, the decisions it made, and the actions it took during the session. You can review exactly what happened and where — see [Test Transcripts](/test-transcripts).

For how Uxia stores and protects the data involved in a test, see [Data Privacy](/account/security/data-privacy).
