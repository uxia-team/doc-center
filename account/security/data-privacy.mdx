Uxia is built to handle sensitive research assets with care. Whether you're uploading design files, sharing live URLs, capturing human participant recordings, or testing behind authenticated flows, every piece of data you bring into the platform is processed with security and privacy as a baseline — not an afterthought. This page explains how your data is handled across the platform.

## Your Design and URL Data

When you upload a design file or provide a URL for a test, Uxia processes that asset securely to run each participant's session.

- **Your data is never used to train models.** Designs, URLs, and test content you submit are not used to improve or fine-tune any AI model — for Uxia or any third party.
- **Your data is not shared with other users or organizations.** Each workspace is isolated; your test assets are only accessible to you and the teammates you explicitly invite.
- **Assets are stored only as long as needed.** Uploaded files and URLs are retained for the duration of the test and to support your ongoing access to results and reports. They are not kept indefinitely beyond what is required to deliver the service.

## Human Participant Recordings

When you invite human participants with a [test link](/test-links) or a [human panel](/human-panel), Uxia records each session: the participant's screen and microphone, plus their face camera if they choose to turn it on. These recordings are:

- **Stored securely** within the Uxia platform and accessible only within your workspace
- **Not shared with third parties** — recordings remain private to your organization
- **Available for review and analysis** inside your workspace dashboard, alongside the AI-generated insights for that test

Recordings give you the qualitative depth to complement the AI-synthesized findings and are retained in your workspace for as long as your account is active.

## Credentials for Gated and Authenticated Tests

If the product or area you're testing sits behind a login screen, you can provide credentials so that AI participants can access protected flows. When you do:

- Credentials are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are decrypted only when an AI participant's session needs to use them
- They're included in the instructions sent to the AI model that operates the participant, only so it can type them into the matching fields, and participants are instructed never to repeat them in their comments
- Password fields stay hidden in transcripts and screenshots, but other typed values, such as a username or email, can be visible
- They stay scoped to the test they were added to and are visible only inside your workspace, never to other organizations
- You remain in control of those credentials and can update or rotate them after each test run if your security policy requires it

Use dedicated test accounts, never real customer or admin accounts. For the full breakdown, see [Tester Credentials](/tester-credentials).

## Files Used During a Test

If your flow asks AI participants to upload something — a CV, an ID, a spreadsheet — you can import files for them to use. When you do:

- Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are retrieved only when an AI participant's session reaches an upload step
- Their contents are never read by an AI model — the file is attached directly to the input, so nothing inside it can appear in transcripts, insights, or reports
- They stay scoped to the test they were imported into and are visible only inside your workspace
- You can remove them at any time

We recommend uploading synthetic documents rather than real ones. See [Tester Files](/tester-files) for the full breakdown.
