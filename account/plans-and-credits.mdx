Uxia has four plans: **Free**, **Starter**, **Pro** and **Custom**. Credits pay for AI participants and accessibility checks. Human participants you invite with a [test link](/test-links) don't use credits.

Your current plan and balance appear in the plan badge at the bottom of the sidebar. Click it to open the plans page, where you can compare plans, see current prices for your region, and upgrade.

## Plans

| Plan | Allowance | New audiences | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free Plan** | Your first test is free | 3 | Sign up |
| **Starter Plan** | 500 credits per month | 10 per month | Existing subscribers only |
| **Pro Plan** | 1,500 credits per month | 15 per month | Upgrade from the plans page, billed monthly or annually |
| **Custom Plan** | Unlimited credits | Unlimited | **Book a demo** on the plans page |

- **Free.** New organizations get one free test instead of credits, within the Free plan limits below. Organizations that joined before the free test was introduced may keep their original balance of 50 credits.
- **Annual billing.** You receive the whole year's allowance at once: 6,000 credits and 120 audiences on Starter, or 18,000 credits and 180 audiences on Pro.
- **Renewals.** At the start of each billing period, your balance is topped back up to the plan allowance. Unused credits don't accumulate on top of it.
- **Plan changes.** When you change plans, your remaining credits are added to the new plan's allowance.
- **Custom.** Adds unlimited credits and audiences, [audience enrichment](/audiences/enrichment), [SSO and SCIM](/account/security/sso), a custom workspace and onboarding, and priority support with live training.

## Credit costs

Credits are charged when you launch a test in the Recruit phase. Before you launch AI participants, the **Credit estimate** panel shows the cost of each block and the **Total credits**.

| Block | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Task (live website, Figma prototype or media prototype) | 20 credits per AI participant, plus 1 credit per AI participant for each benchmark ([SUS](/sus), [UXIA-Q](/uxia-q-score)) you switch on |
| Survey | 1 credit per question per AI participant |
| Card sorting | 10 credits per AI participant |
| Accessibility check | 10 credits per frame (no participants needed) |
| A/B variant (expected to be released in October 2026) | Per-participant costs are doubled, plus 100 credits for the comparison |

Human participants cost nothing in credits when you invite them with a test link. A [human panel](/human-panel) is priced separately: after you send a request, the Uxia team contacts you within 24 hours to discuss pricing.

### Example

A test has a live website task with SUS switched on, followed by a Survey block with 5 questions. You run it with 5 AI participants:

- Task: 5 × (20 + 1) = 105 credits
- Survey: 5 × 5 = 25 credits
- **Total: 130 credits**

## Plan limits

| Limit | Free plan | Paid plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task blocks per test | 1 | 3 |
| Survey blocks per test | 1 | 3 |
| Survey questions per test | 5 | 30 |
| Accessibility check | Not available | 1 block, up to 5 frames |
| Benchmarks (SUS, UXIA-Q) | Not available | Available |
| AI participants per test | 3 | Starter: 5; Pro and Custom: 10 |
| Audiences per test | Up to 3 | Up to 3 |
| Human participants (test links, human panel) | Not available | Available |
| Human sessions shown in results | Not available | Starter: not included; Pro: first 10 per test; Custom: unlimited |
| PDF export | Not available | Pro and Custom |

On the Free plan, parts of the results are locked or limited. You see your top insights, but not their full details or fix suggestions. Survey answers, transcripts, analytics, benchmark scores, audience filters and chat with participants are locked.

## Refunds

- **Stopped tests.** If you stop a test, blocks that hadn't started are refunded 100% and the block that was running is refunded 50%. See [Analyze results](/analyze-results).
- **Blocked AI participants.** If every AI participant is blocked from a live website (for example by anti-bot protection or an access-denied page), 80% of the test's credits are refunded.

## Get free credits

When your organization is on the Free plan and has used its free test, you can earn more through LinkedIn. The offer appears after at least one of your tests has completed successfully. Open it from **Unlock full access** in the sidebar, or from the LinkedIn link under locked results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow Uxia on LinkedIn">
    Follow the Uxia company page and upload a screenshot that shows you follow it. When approved, your organization stays on the Free plan but gets full platform access, including locked results. You still need credits to launch new tests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post about Uxia">
    After the first step is approved, publish a public LinkedIn post about your experience with Uxia. Submit the link to the post and a screenshot of it. When approved, 125 credits are added to your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Uxia team reviews each submission within 24 hours. Each step can be claimed once per organization.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    You can't launch new AI participants until your credits renew, you upgrade, or you earn free credits. Your existing tests and results stay in your workspace. Nothing is deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use more than 3 AI participants on the Free plan?">
    No. The Free plan allows 3 AI participants per test, which is enough to spot the most visible usability issues in a flow. Starter allows 5 per test, and Pro and Custom allow 10.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the Custom plan available for agencies?">
    Yes. The Custom plan suits agencies and research teams that manage several clients or product areas. With unlimited audiences and separate [workspaces](/account/workspaces), you can keep each client's work organized. Email [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app) to discuss a setup for your agency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get a demo or speak with someone at Uxia?">
    Click **Book a demo** on the plans page, visit [uxia.app](https://uxia.app), or email [hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app). The team will schedule a walkthrough and answer your questions about the Custom plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
