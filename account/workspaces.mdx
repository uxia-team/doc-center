Each workspace inside your **Organization** is an isolated hub for projects, tests, audiences, and members. Organization owners manage workspaces from the **Workspaces** tab on the Organization page, and inside any individual workspace your team runs tests and collaborates on research.

## Accessing the Organization page

1. Open the left sidebar.
2. Click your profile or avatar section at the bottom of the sidebar.
3. Select **Organization Settings** from the menu.

<Note>
  Only organization owners can use the Organization page. If the page says you don't have access, ask an owner in your organization to [promote you](/account/people) or make the change for you.
</Note>

## Managing workspaces from the Organization page

The **Workspaces** tab lets organization owners administer all workspaces in one place.

Available actions:

- View all workspaces in the organization.
- See how many workspaces are currently in use compared with your organization limit.
- Create a new workspace with the **Create workspace** button.
- Rename an existing workspace.
- See how many members belong to each workspace.

<Warning>
  If your organization has reached its workspace limit, new workspaces cannot be created until the limit is increased. [Contact us](mailto:hello@uxia.app) to raise it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  A **Delete** option is shown, but workspace deletion is not currently handled directly from this page. Contact Uxia support if you need a workspace removed.
</Note>

## What each workspace includes

Inside a workspace you'll find everything you need to plan, run, and analyze user research:

- **Projects** — folders that group related tests around an initiative, client, or product area. See [Projects](/account/projects).
- **Tests** — every test you've created or run. Open any test to review its results, transcripts, and participant sessions.
- **AI Audience** — reusable audiences that define your AI participants. Build an audience once and use it in any future test. See [Audiences](/audiences/overview).
- **Team members** — invite colleagues to your workspace so they can collaborate on tests and review results (available on the Custom plan).

## Sharing results

After a test completes, Uxia summarizes the findings, patterns, and recommendations in the test's results. You can share them with anyone, including stakeholders who don't have a Uxia account, in two ways:

- **Shareable link** — generate a view-only link and send it directly to teammates or clients.
- **Export** — download the results to share as a file or include in a presentation or research repository.

See [Share and export](/share-and-export) for details.

To follow up an AI study with real people, invite human participants to the same test. See [Test links](/test-links).

## Team access and collaboration

On the Custom plan, you can add teammates to your workspace and collaborate in real time. Team members can:

- View test results
- Access shared audiences and use them in new tests
- Launch tests within the shared workspace environment

To manage workspace-level membership, use the [People page](/account/people) to invite users and assign them to specific workspaces.

<Note>
  Workspace customization — including branded reports, custom workspace settings, and team collaboration — requires the Custom plan. [Contact hello@uxia.app](mailto:hello@uxia.app) or visit [uxia.app](https://uxia.app) to learn more about upgrading.
</Note>

## Custom workspace (Custom plan)

The Custom plan includes a fully branded workspace tailored to your organization:

- **Branded reports** with your company's look and feel, ready to share with clients or internal stakeholders
- **Custom workspace settings** configured to match your team's research workflow
- **Priority support** so your team can move faster and get help when it matters

Custom workspace settings are configured during onboarding with the Uxia team.

## When to contact support

Use the **Contact us** link at the bottom of the Organization page for help with:

- Organization permissions
- People access
- Workspace setup
- Billing or plan limits
- SSO, provisioning, audit logs, or log streaming

## Tips for staying organized

<Tip>
  Name each test with a clear structure — for example, include the product area, the blocks it uses, and the date (e.g., `Checkout flow – Task + Survey – 2026-10`). This makes it easy to find past studies as your workspace grows. Group related tests in a [project](/account/projects), and use separate audiences for each segment, persona, or client so you can reuse them across tests without rebuilding filters each time.
</Tip>
