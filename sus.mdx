The **System Usability Scale (SUS)** is a standardized questionnaire that measures the perceived usability of a digital experience.

After a task, Uxia asks participants the standard SUS statements and combines their answers into a single usability score. This gives you a consistent metric to compare experiences, track improvements over time, and complement the qualitative insights from the test.

## Turn on SUS

On a [Task block](/task-block), switch on **Measure usability with SUS**. Each Task block has its own toggle, so you can measure some flows and not others.

- **Cost:** +1 credit per AI participant, on top of the Task block's 20 credits.
- **Plans:** paid plans only. On the Free plan, the toggle shows an **Upgrade** badge.
- **Results:** the **Benchmarks** tab of the Task block, in the Analyze phase. See [Benchmark results](/benchmark-results).

You can switch on [UXIA-Q](/uxia-q-score) in the same block for another +1 credit per AI participant.

## What does SUS measure?

Participants rate the following 10 statements:

1. I think that I would like to use this product frequently.
2. I found the product unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the product was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to use this product.
5. I found the various functions in this product were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in this product.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use this product very quickly.
8. I found the product very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using the product.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this product.

Each statement is rated on a 5-point scale from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree.

The questionnaire intentionally alternates between positively and negatively worded statements. This helps reduce response patterns.

## Reading the score

The answers are combined into a score from 0 to 100. The industry average is 68, and scores above 80.3 are considered excellent.

## How Uxia applies SUS

Uxia doesn't simply ask AI participants for a score. It applies additional techniques when collecting and processing their answers to reduce common LLM response biases, improve consistency across participants, and increase the accuracy of the final result.
