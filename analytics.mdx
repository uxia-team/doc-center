Where [Insights](/results) tells you what the problems are, the **Analytics** and **Journeys** tabs of a [Task block](/task-block) show how participants actually moved through the flow and where friction happened. Everything here is derived from the participants' [transcripts](/test-transcripts), and it reflects the audiences selected in the results filter.

Use these tabs to answer questions like: How many participants finished? Which screen causes misclicks? How many steps does success usually take? Where do successful and failed paths part ways?

## Analytics

### Summary metrics

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Testers** | Participants with activity in this block, out of those who started the test (for example, **4 / 5**). Participants who didn't complete an earlier required task don't reach this one. |
| **Success Rate** | Share of participants who completed the task |
| **Drop-off** | Share of participants who didn't complete the task |
| **Misclick Rate** | Share of evaluated interactions that didn't activate a valid interactive area |
| **Avg. Number of Steps** | Average steps per participant |
| **Min Number of Steps**, **Max Number of Steps** | The shortest and longest sessions |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How success and drop-off are calculated">
    A participant counts as successful when they complete the task:

    - On **live website** and **Figma prototype** tasks, when they reach the [stop condition](/stop-condition) at any step.
    - On **media prototypes** with an end frame, when their last step lands on that frame.
    - Otherwise, when they reach the end of the session.

    A participant who drops off at any step counts as unsuccessful. **Success Rate** is successful participants divided by **Testers**. **Drop-off** is 100% minus the success rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How misclicks are measured">
    - **Media prototypes (Interactive flow):** of the clicks that could be matched to the screen's interactive areas, the share that missed them.
    - **Figma prototypes:** estimated as the share of clicks after which the screen didn't change. Waits are excluded.
    - **Live websites:** not available, shown as a dash. A click on a real website can change the page in ways that can't be reliably detected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How steps are counted">
    Steps are logical steps, not raw actions. The participant's final reflection isn't counted, and consecutive scrolls in the same direction inside a dropdown or suggestion list count as one step. Average, minimum, and maximum are taken across all participants.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Per-screen analysis

Below the metrics, pick any screen from the thumbnail strip, or use the ← and → keys, to see how participants behaved on it. Screens nobody reached are marked **Not visited**. Click the screen to open a larger preview.

- **Visualization.** Switch between **Heatmap** (where clicks concentrate), **Clickmap** (each click), and **Image** (the plain screen).
- **Path filter.** When some participants succeeded and others didn't, choose **All paths**, **Successful paths**, or **Failed paths** to compare them.
- **Screen metrics.** **Total Testers** (participants who reached the screen), **Total Clicks** (interactions recorded on it), and **Misclick Rate** for that screen.
- **Participant steps.** Each participant's actions and comments on that screen, so you can read why they clicked where they did.

### Export frames

Click the export button next to the visualization menu to open **Export Frames**. Choose an overlay (**Heatmap Overlay**, **Clickmap Overlay**, or **None**), optionally check **Include hotspot areas** to show the prototype's interactive areas, then click **Export** to download the current screen as a PNG, or **Export All (N)** to download every screen as a ZIP file.

## Journeys

The **Journeys** tab shows the routes participants took. Use the **Diagram** / **Paths** toggle to switch views.

- **Diagram.** Columns run from **Start** through **Screen 2**, **Screen 3**, and on. Each node is a screen at that point in the journey, with the number and share of participants who were there (for example, **3 testers (60%)**). Markers show where participants completed the task or dropped off. Hover a node to see who was there, and click it to preview the screen.
- **Paths.** Participants are grouped by the exact sequence of screens they visited, most common first (**Path 1**, **Path 2**, and so on). Each path shows **Success** only if every participant on it completed the task, otherwise **Drop-off** (or **Drop-off \| Access denied**). It also shows the participants who followed it and its **Misclicks**. Click any screen in a path to preview it.

Repeated visits to the same screen in a row are collapsed into one. On live website tasks, the Diagram view may not be available; **Paths** always is.

## What's available for each source

| | Live website | Figma prototype | Media prototype, Interactive flow | Media prototype, Sequential flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Success Rate and Drop-off | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Misclick Rate | No | Estimated | Yes | No |
| Total Clicks per screen | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Steps, heatmaps, and paths | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

For a **Sequential flow** prototype, the unavailable metrics show a dash. Set the prototype up as an **Interactive flow** in the [media canvas](/media-canvas) to get them.
