When you open a Survey block in the **Analyze** phase, its results are split into two tabs:

- **Responses** shows how *everyone* answered each question.
- **Participants** shows how *one* participant answered every question.

## Responses

Each question gets its own card. The left side shows the question number with a type chip, the question text, and any context you added. The right side shows the results, visualized to fit the [question type](/question-types):

| Question type | How it's shown |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | A bar for each option, with option images when you added them |
| Rating scale | A bar for each rating, with your low and high labels |
| Ranking | The items in average rank order, most preferred first |
| Budget allocation | A waffle chart of how the 100 points were split on average |
| Open-ended | Recurring topics, each with its number of mentions and short examples |

Open-ended answers are grouped into topics, so dozens of free-text answers become a handful of recurring ideas instead of a wall of text.

### Go from a number to the people behind it

Every chart is a way in, not a dead end. Click a bar, a rating, or a topic to open a side panel with only the participants behind that answer. It shows how many participants gave that answer, a summary of what they said, and each participant's explanation.

To read every answer to a question, click **See all N responses** under the question. Click **View summary** to return to the chart. On wide screens, the **Questions** index on the right lets you jump between questions.

### Notes you may see

- **"Only testers who completed the mission answered this question."** The question had **Only ask testers who complete the flow** switched on in the [Survey block](/survey-block), so AI participants who didn't complete the task skipped it.
- **"Clusters were calculated from the whole participant group."** You filtered by audience. Topics are still built from all participants, and topics without answers from the filtered audiences are dimmed.
- **No response.** The participant didn't answer, for example because they dropped off before completing the task.

## Participants

The **Participants** tab lets you read one participant's survey from start to finish, the closest thing to sitting with them and going through your questions one by one.

- Pick a participant from the participant list, or use **Search participant** to find someone by name. Each person shows how many questions they answered, such as **4 of 5 answered**.
- Every answer appears in order: the choice, rating, ranking, or allocation, followed by the participant's explanation in their own words.
- Click **View profile** to see who the participant is: their demographics, background, and traits. When an answer surprises you, the profile is the first place to look.
- Click **Chat** to ask the participant follow-up questions. See [Chat with participants](/chat-with-testers).

<Tip>
  To make profiles reflect the people you're building for, use [Audience Enrichment](/audiences/enrichment) to ground your AI participants in your own user research.
</Tip>

## Free plan

On the Free plan, survey responses are locked: charts and participant answers are hidden behind the message "Upgrade your plan to unlock charts and participant answers." Upgrade to a paid plan to see them. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits).

## Human participants

Survey answers from human participants appear in the same **Responses** and **Participants** tabs, with a **Play recording** button that jumps to the moment the participant answered. Chat isn't available for human participants. See [Human participant results](/human-insights).
