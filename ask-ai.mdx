---
title: "Ask AI"
description: "Ask questions about your whole test in plain language and get answers linked to the insights, quotes, and screens behind them."
---

Once your results are in, **Ask AI** lets you question them in plain language instead of combing through every transcript by hand. Ask what the biggest friction points were, why participants dropped off at a particular step, or how one audience fared compared to another, and get an answer drawn from your test's results.

It's most useful for the follow-up questions a results page can't anticipate, especially across blocks: "Do the survey answers back up what happened in the task?"

<Note>
  Ask AI answers questions about the whole test. To talk to one AI participant about their own answers or a specific moment, use [Chat with participants](/chat-with-testers).
</Note>

## Open Ask AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Analyze phase">
    Ask AI becomes available once the test finishes analyzing results. Until then, hovering **Chat** shows "Chat will be available once the test finishes analyzing results."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the sidebar to Chat">
    At the top of the sidebar, switch from **Blocks** to **Chat**. Switch back to **Blocks** at any time to return to your results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your question">
    Type in the box that reads "Ask anything about this test..." and press Enter. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Chat sidebar has a **Start new chat** button and a list of your recent chats. Hover a chat and open its menu to **Rename** or **Delete** it. Deleting a chat is permanent.

## Start from a suggestion

A new chat offers up to four suggestions based on the blocks in your test. The first is always **Summarize this test**. The others ask for the key issues from a Task block, a summary of a Survey block's responses, or the top priorities from an Accessibility check, and **Compare the blocks** when your test has more than one. If tailored suggestions can't load, you see general ones: **Biggest issues**, **Quotes from confused testers**, and **What should we fix first?**

## Follow the evidence

Answers are grounded in your results, and they show their sources. An answer can include cards for:

- **Insights** from a Task block
- **Quotes** from participants
- **Survey questions** and their answers
- **Screens** (frames) from a prototype or website
- **Accessibility criteria** from an Accessibility check

Click a card to jump to that evidence in the results. Under an answer, **Continue** suggests follow-up questions you can send with one click.

If an answer looks wrong or unhelpful, click **Report an issue** below the conversation and tell us what went wrong. If a response stops partway, you see "This conversation was interrupted." Click **Regenerate** to try again.

<Tip>
  Ask AI covers Task, Survey, and Accessibility check blocks. Card sorting results aren't included yet; read them in [Card sorting results](/card-sorting-results).
</Tip>

## Example questions

- "Which participants mentioned pricing, and what did they say?"
- "Summarize the confusion around the checkout step."
- "What did participants with low tech literacy struggle with most?"
- "Do the survey answers back up what happened in the task?"
- "Which accessibility failures should we fix first, and on which screens?"
- "What should we fix before the next round of testing?"

## Availability and limits

| | Free plan | Paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| Ask AI messages | First message on each test is free | Unlimited |
| Chats per test | 1 | Unlimited |

On the Free plan, the chat shows "Your first Ask AI message is free. Upgrade to send more." After that message, upgrade to keep chatting or start another chat. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits).

Ask AI isn't available to people viewing your results through a [shared link](/share-and-export), or on older tests that open their original results. See [What changed with Blocks](/moving-to-blocks).
