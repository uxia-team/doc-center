When a [Task block](/task-block) finishes, open it in the **Analyze** phase to see what participants ran into. The **Insights** tab lists every problem and finding, ranked, and the block header sums up the result in one number: the **Usability Optimization Score (UOS)**.

## The tabs of a Task block

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Insights** | Ranked insights with evidence and fixes (this page) |
| **Transcripts** | Each participant's session, step by step ([Transcripts](/test-transcripts)) |
| **Analytics** | Success, drop-off, misclicks, and heatmaps ([Analytics and Journeys](/analytics)) |
| **Journeys** | The paths participants took ([Analytics and Journeys](/analytics)) |
| **Benchmarks** | SUS and UXIA-Q scores, when switched on ([Benchmark results](/benchmark-results)) |

While the block is running, only **Transcripts** is available, and it fills in live. The other tabs unlock when the run is complete. If every AI participant was blocked from a live website, only **Transcripts** stays available.

## The block header

Click the chevron next to the block title to expand the **Test setup**: the **Scenario**, the **Stop condition** (live website and Figma prototype tasks), the **Product status** (**Finished product** or **Prototype**), and the **Source**. Click the source to open the website in a new tab or to view the prototype frames you tested.

## Usability Optimization Score

The UOS badge, on the right of the block header, shows a score from 0 to 100 in a ring, with a short comment. Click it to read **What is the Usability Optimization Score?** and what your result means.

The score weighs every insight by its relevance and by how many participants ran into it, so it reflects what a typical participant experienced:

- A larger audience surfaces more insights, but doesn't lower the score on its own.
- A longer flow isn't penalized for giving participants more to do.
- Participants who don't complete the task lower the score.
- Insights rated **Prototype** are left out. They point at limits of the prototype, not at your design.

| Comment | What it means |
|---|---|
| **High drop-off** | More than half of the participants didn't complete the task. Review their journeys and your test setup. |
| **Improvement opportunities** | At least one High-relevance insight needs attention. Start there. |
| **Frictions worth fixing** | There are meaningful opportunities to improve the experience. |
| **Minor frictions** | The experience performs well overall, with a few areas to improve. |
| **Strong foundation** | The experience is in good shape, with room to make it even better. |
| **Highly optimized** | Little friction was found in this test. |

When you filter results by audience, the UOS is recalculated for the audiences you selected. Benchmarks (SUS and UXIA-Q) are reported separately and don't feed the UOS.

## The Insights tab

The tab opens with the number of insights reported (for example, **12 insights reported**) and a **Search** box that matches titles, descriptions, and categories. The table has four columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The insight. Click the bookmark to highlight it and move it to the top of the table. |
| **Relevance** | **High**, **Medium**, **Low**, or **Prototype** (may come from a prototype limitation rather than your design). You can change it. |
| **Reporters** | Audience badges and how many participants reported the insight, for example **2 out of 5**. |
| **Category** | The type of problem. Hover it for a definition. |

The categories are **Navigation**, **CTAs**, **Copy**, **Trust**, **Usability**, **Visual Design**, and **Other**. Insights are sorted with highlighted ones first, then by relevance, then by how many participants reported them.

### Change an insight's relevance

Click the relevance of any insight and pick a new value. The badge then shows **Edited**, and the dropdown marks the **(original rating)** so you can switch back. Your change updates the UOS. Rate an insight **Prototype** to take it out of the score.

## Insight details

Click a row to open **Insight details**. The drawer shows the title, relevance, category, reporters, a description, and the affected frames where the problem showed up. Below them, three tabs:

- **Impact.** An **Impact Assessment** split into **User Impact** and **Business Impact**.
- **Evidence.** **What Testers Said**: quotes grouped by participant. Click a quote to jump to where the participant said it. When the insight is about behavior rather than words, you see **What Testers Did** instead, with the participants it was **Observed in**.
- **Fix.** A **Fix Suggestion** with its **Acceptance criteria**, followed by a **Prompt** you can **Copy** or **Expand** and paste into an AI coding tool. Older results show a **Technical Complexity** rating instead: **Quick fix**, **Requires planning**, or **Major redesign**.

<Tip>
  To pull insights into Claude Code, Cursor, or another AI tool without copy-pasting, connect the [Uxia MCP server](/integrations/mcp).
</Tip>

## Free plan limits

On the Free plan, the Insights tab shows the top insights only (up to 5) and tells you how many are hidden: **You are missing +N insights**. The description, affected frames, and **Impact** tab stay visible; **Evidence** and **Fix** are locked. Paid plans see everything. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits).

## Human participants

These tabs show AI participant results. To see what human participants did on the same task, switch the participant type to **Human participants**. See [Human participant results](/human-insights) and [Analyze your results](/analyze-results).
