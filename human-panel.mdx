Don't have participants of your own? Request a **human panel**, and our team recruits screened participants that match the audience you describe. Pricing is agreed with our team directly; it isn't charged in credits.

The human panel is available on paid plans. It isn't available for tests that only contain an [Accessibility check](/accessibility-block), because those don't need participants.

## Request a panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the [Recruit](/recruit-participants) phase, click **Human panel**. The form reads *"Define the audience you would like us to recruit for your test."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your participants">
    Fill in the fields below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Click **Send Request**. Someone from our team will contact you within 24 hours to discuss pricing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| **Number of testers** | How many participants you need. |
| **Age range** | **Any**, or clear **Any** to set a **Minimum age** and **Maximum age** between 18 and 100. |
| **Gender** | **Any**, **Female**, **Male**, or **Evenly distributed**. |
| **Language** | Required. English, Spanish, Korean, German, French, Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal), or Italian. Test instructions match the language you choose, so pick the language your participants speak. |
| **Country of residence** | **Any**, or clear **Any** to search for a country. |
| **Additional details** | Optional. Anything else we should screen for. |

<Tip>
  Use **Additional details** for screening criteria the other fields don't cover. For example: *"Orders from a food-delivery app at least once a week. No one who works in tech or marketing."* Specific criteria get you closer to your real users.
</Tip>

## After you send it

The form switches to a summary of your requirements, with the message **Request sent** and the date you submitted it. In the Recruit summary, a **Human panel** row shows the number of participants, **Request sent**, the status **Requested**, and **Pricing by our team** in place of credits.

To change your requirements, click **Update request**, edit the fields, and click **Send updated request**. Our team gets the new version.

If the request was saved but our team couldn't be notified, the form says so. Click **Retry sending request**.

## Combine with other methods

A human panel works alongside [AI participants](/ai-participants) and your own [test links](/test-links) in the same test. Human participants' sessions appear in Analyze under **Human participants**, separate from AI results.
