A test is a sequence of **blocks**. Participants go through the blocks in order — for example, a task on your signup flow, then a survey about the experience. You build the test in the **Build** phase, then move on to **Recruit**.

## Create a test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create dialog">
    Click **Create test** from the sidebar, the Home page, the **Tests** page, or inside a project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Unmoderated test">
    Pick **Unmoderated test** — participants complete the test on their own, and you get insights, paths, and clicks. (AI-moderated tests are [coming soon](/ai-moderated-tests).)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a project">
    Enter a **Title** (up to 72 characters) and choose a **Project**, or **No project**. You can also create a new project from here. Click **Create test** to open the builder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add blocks

In an empty test, the **Add a block** picker opens automatically. Later, use **Add new block** at the end of the canvas, or **Add new block here** between two blocks to insert one in a specific position.

| Section | Block | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Task** | **Live website task** | Test a live website, platform, or coded prototype by URL |
| | **Figma prototype task** | Test a Figma prototype by its share link |
| | **Media prototype task** | Build and test a prototype from your own images |
| **Research** | **Survey** | Ask questions with different question types |
| | **Card sorting** | See how participants group and label your content |
| **Audits** | **Accessibility check** | Check your screens against WCAG 2.2 AA or AAA |

Each block has its own page: [Task block](/task-block), [Survey block](/survey-block), [Card sorting block](/card-sorting), and [Accessibility check block](/accessibility-block).

<Tip>
  When you add a second block of the same type, Uxia pre-fills its source (URL or prototype) from the previous one, so testing several flows of the same product takes seconds.
</Tip>

### Limits per test

| | Free plan | Paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| Task blocks (all three types combined) | 1 | 3 |
| Survey blocks | 1 | 3 |
| Survey questions (across the test) | 5 | 30 |
| Accessibility check blocks | Not available | 1 (up to 5 frames) |
| Card sorting blocks | No limit | No limit |

When you reach a limit, the block is disabled in the picker. On the Free plan, locked blocks show an **Upgrade** badge.

## Organize your blocks

The left sidebar lists every block in order, with its title and a short summary — the task text, or the number of questions, cards, or frames.

- **Reorder** by dragging a block in the sidebar, or use **Move up** / **Move down** from the block's **…** menu.
- **Duplicate** a block from the **…** menu to copy it with all its settings.
- **Delete** a block from the **…** menu.

<Warning>
  Deleting a block is immediate and can't be undone.
</Warning>

Your changes are saved automatically as you work.

### Order matters

Participants take the blocks in the order you set. Two settings depend on it:

- In a Survey block placed after a task, each question can be limited to AI participants who completed the task, with **Only ask testers who complete the flow**. This is how you ask post-task questions. See [Survey block](/survey-block).
- A live website or Figma task that uses the same URL as an earlier task can **Continue from where participants left the previous task**, so participants pick up in the same browser session.

## Fix issues before recruiting

Required fields are checked as you work. When a block is missing something — a URL, a task, a scenario, a question — it's marked with a red alert icon in the sidebar and the field shows what's needed, such as *"You need to describe the task"*.

The red issues counter in the header lists every open issue by block. Click an item to jump straight to it.

## Start recruiting

When your blocks are ready, click **Start recruiting**. Uxia checks every block; if something is missing, it takes you to the first issue. Otherwise, the **Recruit** phase opens, where you choose your participants.

<Note>
  You can keep editing blocks until you launch AI participants. Before AI participants start, Uxia runs a quick [test review](/recruit-participants#test-review) to catch unclear tasks or questions.
</Note>
