Test links bring your own participants into a test: customers, users, a panel you manage, or colleagues. Send the link through any channel, such as email, Slack, or a newsletter. Participants don't need an account, and human participants cost 0 credits.

Test links are available on paid plans. Participants take the same blocks as your [AI participants](/ai-participants), so you can compare both groups in [Analyze](/analyze-results).

<Note>
  On the Starter plan, you can create test links, but human results aren't included. Pro shows the first 10 sessions per test, and Custom shows all of them. See [Human participant results](/human-insights#plan-limits).
</Note>

## Create a test link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Test links">
    In the [Recruit](/recruit-participants) phase, click **Test links**, then **Create link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the link">
    In **Create Test Link**, give the link a **Link name**, such as *Newsletter audience*, and choose the settings below. Then click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Click **Copy link** and send it to your participants. Use **Preview** to try the participant experience yourself; preview sessions aren't saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Create one link per group or channel, up to 10 links per test. Each link has its own settings, and in Analyze you can filter results by link.

## Link settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Link name** | Identifies the link in Recruit and in the Analyze filter. Required. |
| **Show interactive areas after an invalid click** | Highlights the clickable areas when a participant clicks outside them. Shown for media prototypes with interactive areas. On by default. |
| **Face camera** | *"Screen recording is always required. Enable this to also require face camera permission before participants can start."* |
| **Default language** | The language participants see. Uxia translates your tasks, scenarios, and questions automatically. Choose from English, Spanish, Korean, German, French, Chinese, Português (Brasil), Português (Portugal), and Italian. |
| **Participants can choose language in the session** | Adds a language selector to the welcome screen. |

If available in your workspace, **Allow 3rd Party human participants** lets participants from an external recruiting panel register their completion. Paste your recruiter's **Completion link**, and participants see a **Confirm your participation** button when they finish.

## Manage your links

The **Test links** table shows each link's **Link**, **Default language**, **Status**, and **Face camera** setting. From a link's **…** menu:

- **Stop** closes the link. Participants who open it see *"This test is unavailable"* and *"No longer accepting responses."*
- **Activate** reopens a stopped link.
- **Edit settings** changes the link's settings. Changes apply to future participants.

If your test has [A/B variants](/ab-variants), each link has one URL per variant.

## The participant experience

Participants need a desktop browser that supports screen recording.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome and consent">
    Participants pick a language, if the link allows it. They agree to the Terms & Conditions and confirm that their screen, microphone, and camera may be recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Before you begin">
    They read two short instructions: **Think out loud**, and **Device & permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Device check">
    They turn on their microphone and say a few words to test it, plus their camera if **Face camera** is on. Then they click **Start test** and share their screen. Tests with a live website task require sharing the entire screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The blocks">
    Participants go through your blocks in order. For a task, they read the brief and click **Start task**; a live website opens in another tab. They click **Finish task** when done, or **Abandon task** to give up. Surveys and benchmarks are answered on screen, and card sorts are drag and drop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test completed">
    The recording uploads, and participants see **Test completed**. With a completion link, they also see **Confirm your participation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Good to know:

- Sessions are capped at 60 minutes. Participants are warned at 5 minutes and 1 minute left, then the session submits automatically.
- Accessibility check blocks are skipped. They don't need participants.
- The same browser can't submit a test link twice. A second attempt shows *"This test was already submitted."*
- On the Custom plan, the Uxia logo is hidden from the session.

## What gets recorded

- **Screen recording** of the test as the participant sees it.
- **Microphone audio** of their spoken thoughts.
- **Face camera**, when required by the link.
- **Clicks and navigation** in your prototype.
- **Transcripts**, generated automatically from the audio.

<Tip>
  In your invitation, ask participants to use a desktop computer, tell them roughly how long the test takes, and suggest a quiet place to think out loud. It saves you from failed and silent recordings.
</Tip>
