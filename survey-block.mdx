A **Survey block** asks participants questions directly: about your product, your messaging, your concepts, or anything else you need to understand. Instead of navigating an interface, participants answer in their own words, so you learn the *why* behind their behavior.

## Surveys and tasks work together

A [Task block](/task-block) and a Survey block measure different things:

- **Task blocks are behavioral.** They show how participants *navigate and interact* with your product, and where they get stuck.
- **Survey blocks are attitudinal.** They capture what participants *think, feel, and prefer*.

Use surveys to validate ideas and understand opinions, and tasks to find UX friction. You don't have to choose: a single test can include both, so you can watch participants complete a checkout and then ask them what they thought of it.

## When to use a Survey block

- **Validate concepts and ideas.** Pressure-test feature ideas and value propositions before you commit design or engineering time. Describe the idea in plain language, or attach a rough concept as an image.
- **Test messaging and copy.** Put headlines, feature names, labels, or pricing framing head to head, and check whether your terminology means what you think it means.
- **Understand your audience.** Compare how different audiences answer the same question, and uncover unmet needs and the language your users bring.
- **Explain the why behind your data.** When analytics or a task show you *what* happens but not *why*, ask participants about the motivations behind the behavior.

## Add a Survey block

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the block">
    In the **Build** phase, click **Add new block** (or **Add block** in the sidebar) and choose **Survey** under **Research**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Click **Add question** and pick a type: **Open-ended**, **Multiple choice**, **Rating scale**, **Ranking**, or **Budget allocation**. See [Question types](/question-types) for how to set up each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine each question">
    Write the question text, then add answer options or scale labels if the type needs them. Click **+ Add more context** to give participants extra information or reference images.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every question is required, and every answer comes with a detailed explanation of the reasoning behind it.

## Ask follow-up questions after a task

To ask questions after participants finish a task (what used to be called post-test questions), add a Survey block **after** a Task block. AI participants answer with their experience of the task in mind, so you can ask things like:

- "Was there any point where you weren't sure what to do next?"
- "Did you miss any information on the product details page?"
- "How appealing did you find the price?"

Questions in a survey placed after a Task block show an **Only ask testers who complete the flow** switch. It is off by default. Turn it on for questions that only make sense if the participant reached the end of the task, such as how easy it was to complete the order. AI participants who didn't complete the task skip that question. Human participants answer every question.

<Tip>
  Keep follow-up questions open where you want texture, and specific where you're chasing a known concern.
</Tip>

## Limits and credits

| | Free plan | Paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| Survey blocks per test | 1 | 3 |
| Questions per test (across all Survey blocks) | 5 | 30 |
| Credits | 1 per question per AI participant | 1 per question per AI participant |

For example, a survey with 8 questions run with 10 AI participants costs 80 credits. Human participants who join through [test links](/test-links) cost no credits. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits).

## What you get

AI participants answer right away, with no recruiting, scheduling, or incentives. You see every answer in their own words, charts for every question, and recurring topics in open-ended answers. See [Survey results](/survey-results).

Because AI participants stay available after the test, you can [chat with any of them](/chat-with-testers) to follow up on an answer. To ground their answers in your real users rather than a generic population, use [Audience Enrichment](/audiences/enrichment): share your personas, interviews, or research, and Uxia creates AI participants who think and respond like the people you want to reach.
